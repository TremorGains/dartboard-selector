Contact us

Questions, bugs, ideas or privacy requests — all welcome.

Email

hello@dartpick.app

We usually reply within a few days, and always within 30 days for privacy requests.

Reporting a bug

It helps to include:

  • the device and browser you’re using (for example “Pixel 8, Chrome”),
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead,
  • a screenshot, if you can.

Please don’t include personal information we don’t need.

Privacy requests

DartPick keeps everything you add on your own device, so the quickest way to see or remove your data is Your data in the app: download a copy, or delete everything instantly. For anything else — questions about the privacy policy or a request we can help with — email us and we’ll respond within 30 days.
