Privacy policy

Version 1.1 · Last updated 16 September 2026

The short version

DartPick doesn’t collect your personal information. Everything you add — names, pictures, where you pin them and your settings — is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

DartPick is made by Ryan Faraone, an independent developer based in Australia. You can reach us at hello@dartpick.app.

What stays on your device

  • The names and pictures you add, and where you pin them on the board.
  • Your settings, such as sound on or off and the board’s arrangement.

This is kept in your browser’s storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). It is never sent to us or anyone else. It’s removed when you choose Your data → Delete everything, clear this site’s data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

What leaves your device

  • Loading the website. When your browser loads DartPick, our hosting provider, GitHub Pages (run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States), receives technical details such as your IP address and browser type so it can deliver the site and keep it secure. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • Emails you send us. We use them only to reply to you, and delete them once they’re no longer needed or when you ask.
  • Ads. DartPick doesn’t show ads yet. If that changes, we’ll update this policy before any ads appear and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

  • See or download your data: open Your data → Download my data for a file with everything DartPick has stored on your device.
  • Delete your data: Your data → Delete everything removes it from your device straight away.
  • Ask us anything: email hello@dartpick.app. We’ll reply within 30 days. Because we don’t hold personal information about you on our side, most requests are about data on your device, which you control directly.

Children

DartPick is intended for people aged 13 and over. It isn’t designed for children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Security

DartPick is served only over HTTPS. Your data stays in your browser’s storage, so anyone who can use your device or browser profile can also see it.

International transfers

Our hosting provider, GitHub, is based in the United States, so the technical details above are processed there under GitHub’s own privacy commitments.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we’ll post the new version here with a new date, and point out significant changes in the app.

Questions and complaints

Please contact us first at hello@dartpick.app. If you’re not satisfied, you can voice your concerns to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), or, if you live in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.
