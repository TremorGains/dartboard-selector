About DartPick

A playful way to pick from a list.

What it is

DartPick is an alternative to name spinners. Instead of watching a wheel slow down, you pin names or pictures to a dartboard as sticky notes and throw a dart. The winner is chosen fairly before the dart flies, and the result pops up with a little celebration. It’s for deciding turns, drawing raffles, picking a movie — any time a group needs a fair, fun choice.

Who makes it

DartPick is made by an independent developer based in Australia (publisher name to be added before public launch). It started as a small personal project and grew into a full app. Feedback and ideas are always welcome.

Privacy first

There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you add stays on your own device, and you can download or delete it at any time from Your data. The privacy policy explains exactly what happens to your information.
