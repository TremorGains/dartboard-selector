About DartPick

A playful way to pick from a list.

What it is

DartPick is an alternative to name spinners. Instead of watching a wheel slow down, you pin names or pictures to a dartboard as sticky notes and throw a dart. The winner is chosen fairly before the dart flies, and the result pops up with a little celebration. It’s for deciding turns, drawing raffles, picking a movie — any time a group needs a fair, fun choice.

Who makes it

DartPick is made by Ryan Faraone, an independent developer based in Australia. It started as a small personal project and grew into a full app. Feedback and ideas are always welcome.

Privacy first

There are no accounts and no analytics. Everything you add stays on your own device, and you can download or delete it at any time from Your data. Ads from Google AdSense will pay for DartPick once the site is approved, and they’ll be plain ads unless you choose otherwise — your names, pictures and boards are never used for them. The privacy policy explains exactly what happens to your information.
